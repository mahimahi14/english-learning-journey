The main rule is:

Words like hundred, thousand, million, dozen, etc. remain singular when they are preceded by a specific number/numeral.

1. Number + hundred/thousand/million

When a specific number comes before these words, don't add -s.

✅ Five thousand rupees
❌ Five thousands rupees

✅ Two thousand people
❌ Two thousands people

✅ Three million dollars
❌ Three millions dollars

2. When do we use the plural form?

When we are talking about an unspecified large quantity, we use:

thousands of
hundreds of
millions of

Examples:

Thousands of people were present.

Hundreds of students attended the event.

Millions of people use the internet.



3. Dozen

The same idea applies to dozen.

Specific number → singular "dozen"

She bought two dozen eggs. ✅

He purchased three dozen bananas. ✅

❌ She bought two dozens eggs.

Unspecified quantity → plural "dozens"

She bought dozens of eggs. ✅

There were dozens of people waiting outside. ✅
