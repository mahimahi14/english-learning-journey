                                              1. Either ... or
Either...or is used to present two alternatives.

Either Ram or Shyam is responsible.

Either the teacher or the students are responsible.

Important Rule ⭐

When two subjects are joined by either...or, the verb generally agrees with the subject nearer to the verb.

Either Ram or his friends are coming.
→ nearer subject = friends → are

Either his friends or Ram is coming.
→ nearer subject = Ram → is
                                               2.Neither.....nor


Neither...nor is used to express a negative choice between two alternatives.

Neither Ram nor Shyam is present.

Neither the teacher nor the students are present.

Neither the colour nor the size of the clothes suits me. ✅(Here the nearer subject is the size, which is singular, so suits is used also here size is considered not the clothes,so as size is singluar subject,suits is used)

                                                  3. Not only ... but also

Not only...but also is used to add/emphasize two things or people.

Not only Ram but also his friends were present.(can also write like this.....Not only Ram but his friends were also present)

Not only his friends but also Ram was present.
